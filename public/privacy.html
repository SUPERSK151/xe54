Privacy notice

How we handle your personal information under the Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA).

What we collect

When you submit a form on this website we collect the details you enter: your name, email address, phone number, organisation and the information in your message or application.

Why we collect it

We use your information only to respond to your enquiry, assess investor access requests, consider job applications and manage partnership discussions.

Who sees it

Only authorised XE 54 staff. We use an email delivery provider to send and receive form messages. We do not sell your information or share it for marketing.

How long we keep it

Enquiries are kept for as long as needed to deal with them. Job applications are kept for up to 12 months unless you ask us to delete them sooner.

Your rights

You may ask to see, correct or delete your personal information, or object to how we process it, by emailing skshange@xe54intelligence.co.za. You may also complain to the Information Regulator of South Africa.
