The challenge

Security that depends on someone else isn't secure.

Much of South Africa's advanced defence technology is imported. That leaves forces exposed to supply-chain delays, sanctions risk and equipment that sits unserviceable while waiting for foreign parts.

  • Fragmented communications. Agencies often run incompatible systems, which slows joint operations.
  • Foreign dependency. Few local suppliers can deliver integrated systems end to end.
  • Training and border gaps. Specialised intelligence and counter-threat skills are in short supply across the region.
