About XE 54

Sovereign capability,
built in South Africa.

XE 54 Intelligence Solutions is a 100% South African-owned company headquartered in Pretoria. We are building a vertically integrated business that spans defence manufacturing, security services and intelligence training.

Our mission

Close the gap between intelligence and action.

We believe Africa's security should rest on technology, skills and support that are owned and maintained on the continent.

Our model keeps research, production, integration, training and aftermarket support under one roof. That means faster delivery, lower lifetime cost and systems that keep working because the people who built them are close by.

What sets us apart

Four principles behind everything we build.

01

Sovereign ownership

Fully South African owned and operated, with intellectual property retained locally.

02

Vertical integration

End-to-end capability across R&D, manufacturing, systems integration, training and support, without dependence on foreign subcontractors.

03

Local manufacturing

In-country production that reduces import dependency, protects against supply-chain disruption and creates skilled local jobs.

04

Built for African conditions

Systems designed for African terrain, climate and cross-border operating environments, with support teams in the region.

Compliance

Responsible by design.

Defence and security work in South Africa is strictly regulated. XE 54 designs its products, services and exports to comply with South African law, including the requirements of the National Conventional Arms Control Committee (NCACC), defence procurement rules and the Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA).

We work only with legitimate government agencies, regulated security providers and lawful partners.
